Quiz

Test yourself on the guide's 51 techniques, free, in your browser and offline.

Test yourself on the 51 techniques in the guide. You are shown a real-life example and choose which technique it is. After each answer you see the definition, the protective response and a caution. The quiz runs in your browser, works offline once it has been opened, and keeps your results on your device. There are no accounts and no tracking.

Start the quiz Offline and downloads Home

How it works

  1. Press Start a quiz, then choose how many questions you want. You can also limit the quiz to some categories or groups.
  2. Pick the technique that fits the example. You see straight away whether you were right, with the full entry for that technique.
  3. At the end you see your score and can retry the ones you missed. Your history stays in this browser only.

If you would rather practise the other way round (given the technique, pick the example), change the question and answer columns on the setup screen.

Good to know

  • The quiz uses the same wording as the guide. The questions come from a spreadsheet you can download, licensed CC BY-SA 4.0.
  • The quiz engine is the open-source Sheet2Quiz app (MIT licence), fixed on this site to the guide's own questions. For your own offline copy or your own questions, see Downloads.
  • The quiz has a Home button and a Leave site button, like every page here.
