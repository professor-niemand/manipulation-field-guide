Sources and further reading

The sources behind the guide, with the books we recommend first.

Further Reading

Entries marked with an asterisk (*) are recommended as first reads.

  • *Cialdini, Influence: The Psychology of Persuasion (1984; revised edition 2021). The classic on why people say yes.

  • *Pratkanis & Aronson, Age of Propaganda (revised edition 2001). Everyday persuasion and propaganda, broken down technique by technique.

  • *Milgram, Obedience to Authority (1974). The obedience experiments, told by the man who ran them.

  • *Walker, Complex PTSD: From Surviving to Thriving (2013). The most accessible route into the fawn response and recovery from long-term manipulation.

  • Herman & Chomsky, Manufacturing Consent (1988). The foundational text on media gatekeeping and propaganda. Denser than the rest.

  • Freyd & Birrell, Blind to Betrayal (2013). The reader-friendly account of DARVO and institutional betrayal.
