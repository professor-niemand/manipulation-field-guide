## How to use the library

1. Download the library file above. Keep it as an `.xlsx` file.
2. Open the quiz app once while you are online. After that it works offline, and your browser can install it to your home screen.
3. On the app's home screen choose **Import library** and pick the file you downloaded.
4. On the setup screen set **Question column** to *Example* and **Answer column** to *Technique*. You can also filter by *Category* or *Group*, choose how many questions you want, and choose which columns appear on the card after each answer.
5. To use it offline later, open the app from your home screen or browser as normal. Your imported library stays on your device.

## About the app and the library

The quiz app is a separate open-source project under the MIT licence. The library is licensed like the guide, CC BY-SA 4.0. The library has one row per technique, so you can also open it in any spreadsheet program and use it for your own revision.
