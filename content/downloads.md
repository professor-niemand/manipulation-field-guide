## Use the quiz offline {#offline}

For offline use, the quiz app is **[Sheet2Quiz](%%QUIZ_URL%%)**, the free, open-source app (MIT licence) that the quiz on this site is built from. You install it once, import the library file above, and after that it works without a connection. Everything stays on your device. The code is in the [Sheet2Quiz repository](%%QUIZ_REPO%%).

**Step 1: install the app (needs a connection once)**

- **Android phone or tablet (Chrome):** open [Sheet2Quiz](%%QUIZ_URL%%), let it load, open the browser menu (the three dots) and choose **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
- **iPhone or iPad (Safari):** open [Sheet2Quiz](%%QUIZ_URL%%) in Safari, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**. Safari is the most reliable browser for this on Apple devices.
- **Windows, Mac or Linux (Chrome or Edge):** open [Sheet2Quiz](%%QUIZ_URL%%) and click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, or open the menu and choose **Install**.
- **Safari on a Mac:** choose **File**, then **Add to Dock**.
- **Firefox on a computer** cannot install web apps, but Sheet2Quiz still works offline in a normal tab once you have opened it, as long as you do not clear the browser's site data.

**Step 2: download the library**

Press **Download library** above and keep the file as an `.xlsx` file. On a phone, note where the file is saved (usually the Downloads folder).

**Step 3: import it**

1. In Sheet2Quiz, choose **Import library** and pick the file you downloaded.
2. On the setup screen set **Question column** to *Example* and **Answer column** to *Technique*. You can also filter by *Category* or *Group*, choose how many questions you want, and choose which columns appear on the card after each answer.
3. Press **Start quiz**.

**Check that it works offline.** Switch on flight mode (or disconnect from the internet) and open the app from your home screen or browser. If your library is listed, offline mode is working.

**Good to know**

- Your libraries and results are saved in the browser on that device only. Clearing the browser's site data deletes them, and private or incognito windows do not keep them. The app has **Back up** and **Restore** buttons.
- Sheet2Quiz works with any `.xlsx` or `.csv` file, so you can make your own libraries for any subject. Put your column names in the first row and one card per row.
- The [quiz on this site](quiz.html) also works offline once it has been opened, but it only contains this guide's questions.
- **An installed app leaves an icon on your home screen or desktop.** If someone else may look at your device, do not install it. Use it in a browser tab instead, and see the [privacy notes](get-help.html#staying-private-online).
