## Use the quiz offline {#offline}

The [quiz](quiz.html) is a small web app. Open it once while you are online. After that it works without a connection, and you can install it so it opens like an ordinary app. You do not need to download any file for this. The questions are already inside it.

**Android phone or tablet (Chrome)**

1. Open the [quiz](%%QUIZ_APP%%) and let it load.
2. Open the browser menu (the three dots) and choose **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
3. Open it from your home screen whenever you like, online or not.

**iPhone or iPad (Safari)**

1. Open the [quiz](%%QUIZ_APP%%) in Safari and let it load.
2. Tap the **Share** button, then **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Open it from your home screen. Safari is the most reliable browser for this on Apple devices.

**Windows, Mac or Linux computer**

- **Chrome or Edge:** open the [quiz](%%QUIZ_APP%%) and click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, or open the menu and choose **Install**.
- **Safari on a Mac:** choose **File**, then **Add to Dock**.
- **Firefox on a computer** cannot install web apps, but the quiz still works offline in a normal tab once you have opened it, as long as you do not clear the browser's site data.

**Check that it works offline.** After opening the quiz once, switch on flight mode (or disconnect from the internet) and open it again. If the first screen appears, offline mode is working.

**Good to know**

- Your results are saved in the browser on that device only. Clearing the browser's site data deletes them, and private or incognito windows do not keep them. The quiz has a **Back up history** button if you want a copy.
- When the guide is updated, the quiz updates itself the next time you open it while online.
- **An installed app leaves an icon on your home screen or desktop.** If someone else may look at your device, do not install it. Use it in a browser tab instead, and see the [privacy notes](get-help.html#staying-private-online).

## Make your own quizzes

The quiz on this site only uses the guide's questions. The app it is built from, [Sheet2Quiz](%%QUIZ_URL%%), is free, open source (MIT licence) and works with any spreadsheet. If you want to build libraries for your own revision, it reads `.xlsx` and `.csv` files:

1. Make a spreadsheet with your column names in the first row, for example *Question* and *Answer*, and one card per row.
2. Open Sheet2Quiz, choose **Import library** and pick your file.
3. On the setup screen choose which column is the question and which is the answer. You can also filter by a category column and choose how many questions to ask.
4. It works offline in the same way as above, and everything stays on your device.

You can also import this guide's library file into Sheet2Quiz, or open it in a spreadsheet program and use it as a starting point for your own.
